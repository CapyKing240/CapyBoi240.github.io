Where We’ve Been: Rebranding, New Prototypes & Taking a Step Back

If you noticed silence on our devlogs and social channels over the last 3 to 4 months, you weren't imagining things. As an independent team, it's easy to get caught in a cycle of pushing updates just for the sake of staying visible. But behind the scenes, we realized our internal workflow, project structures, and brand identity were no longer keeping pace with where we wanted to take Moonlight Studios.

Clearing the Technical & Design Debt

Instead of rushing out half-baked updates across multiple fronts, we chose to hit pause on public releases. We spent this time standardizing our design systems, and completely overhauling our digital footprint. Transitioning to our new identity and launching our new site at moonlightstudios.net wasn't just a visual facelift; it was a necessary reset to ensure everything we release moving forward meets a much higher standard of polish.

Experimental Pipelines & What’s Ahead

During this quiet period, we’ve also been quietly prototyping new 2D mechanics and exploring fresh game concepts. While these projects are still in their infancy and far from reveal-ready, taking the time to experiment without the pressure of an immediate deadline has given us renewed clarity. Now that our core infrastructure and rebrand are fully locked in, we're shifting our entire focus back to active development and consistent community updates.
