About the Game

Wizardsbane is a fast-paced 2D pixel-art metroidvania where exploration, movement, and combat all lead to one final showdown. After the death of the castle's lord, a mysterious wizard seized the fortress, corrupting its halls with dark magic and unleashing monsters upon the nearby town of Grayhaven. Every hero who entered the castle vanished, leaving you—a fallen knight with a troubled past—as the town's last hope.

Explore a vast interconnected castle filled with hidden paths, dangerous enemies, and forgotten secrets. Unlock new movement abilities to reach unexplored areas, discover shortcuts, and overcome increasingly difficult challenges. Along the way, you'll meet survivors, traders, and other mysterious characters, each with their own stories to tell, before facing the wizard in a final battle that puts everything you've learned to the test.

Key Features

  • Explore a connected world — Discover hidden passages, shortcuts, secrets, and interconnected areas throughout the castle.
  • Unlock new abilities — Gain powerful movement upgrades like dashes, wall jumps, double jumps, and more to expand your exploration.
  • Fast, skill-based combat — Learn enemy patterns, master your abilities, and overcome challenging encounters.
  • An epic final showdown — Everything leads to one intense battle against the wizard who corrupted the castle.
