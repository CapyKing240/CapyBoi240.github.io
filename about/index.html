About us

We are a small indie team brought together by a shared love for making games.

Moonlight Studios started back in 2021. At the beginning, we were just testing out wild ideas, learning how to make games, and putting together small prototypes for game jams. That experimentation gave us the spark we needed, and we decided to form a real team. Shortly after, we released our first official title, Super Pet Fighter.

As a young studio working with limited resources, we leaned heavily on passion, creativity, and steady learning. Over time, as our skills improved, our vision grew too. Today, we are tackling larger, more ambitious projects while staying true to what brought us together in the first place: making original, smooth games that players all over the world can pick up and enjoy.
