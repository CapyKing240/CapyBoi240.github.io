Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Moonlight Studios ("we", "us") collects through moonlightstudios.net, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Messages you send us. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic and message you type in. The form is handled by a service called Formspree, which passes your message on to our studio inbox.

Basic technical data. Like every website, the servers that deliver this site (GitHub Pages) may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We don't have access to a personal profile built from this.

We don't run advertising, we don't sell your data, and we don't use analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to the messages you send us
  • To keep the website running and secure

We don't use your email address for newsletters or marketing unless you clearly ask us to.

Services that load on our pages

  • Google Fonts loads the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google when a page opens.
  • YouTube videos on game pages and devlogs use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube only gets involved if you press play.
  • Formspree processes the contact form when you send a message.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

These services have their own privacy policies, and we don't control how they handle data.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to itch.io, YouTube, Discord, X and other sites. Their privacy practices are their own. Please read their policies before you share personal information with them. When you play our games on itch.io, itch.io's policies apply to your account and downloads.

How long we keep your messages

We keep messages for as long as we need them to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact page and tell us what you'd like. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law, and we will respect them.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact page.
