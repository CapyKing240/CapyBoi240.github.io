About Moonlight Studios

We're a small indie studio creating memorable games with passion and creativity.

Moonlight Studios is a small independent game development studio driven by a love for creating games that are fun, memorable, and full of personality. What started as a passion for making games has grown into a studio that's always experimenting with new ideas, improving our skills, and taking on bigger challenges with every project.

Over the years, we've created games like Clonescape, Super Pet Fighter, and Wizardsbane, each one teaching us something new and helping us grow as developers. Every project is an opportunity to learn, try new mechanics, and create experiences that players can genuinely enjoy.

We believe that great games come from attention to detail. Whether it's satisfying gameplay, smooth controls, expressive visuals, or the little touches players might not notice at first, we care about making every part of the experience feel polished. We spend a lot of time refining our games because we know those small details can make a big difference.

Right now, we're working on new titles while continuing to expand our ambitions. As our experience grows, so do our projects. Our goal is simple: keep making better games, explore new ideas, and build worlds that players want to come back to.

We're still a small team, but that's something we're proud of. It gives us the freedom to experiment, stay creative, and put genuine care into everything we create. This is only the beginning, and we're excited to see where the journey takes us next.
