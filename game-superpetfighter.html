Super Pet Fighter

Explore, train, and duel! Choose your companion and battle over 15 rival pets to become the ultimate champion in Super Pet Fighter: Re-hamstered Edition—available now!

Play on itch.io →

About the Game

Super Pet Fighter: Re-hamstered Edition brings you into the world of New Roshamburg City, where the ultimate pet fighting championship is about to begin. Enter a vibrant, interactive world full of colorful characters, hidden secrets, and tough opponents waiting around every corner. Choose your starter companion, explore the town, and battle your way through the ranks using strategy, timing, and the classic game of Rock-Paper-Scissors—better known around here as Rachambo.

From cozy city streets to hidden training grounds, you'll travel through various areas, bond with your companion, and take on over 15 unique rival pets, each with their own specialized abilities and playstyles. Train hard, guide your pet through tough decisions, and rise to become the ultimate champion—or fail to prepare and risk getting taken straight to the pound!

Key Features

  • A World to Explore — Wander through New Roshamburg City, talk to locals, interact with the environment, and discover new challenges.
  • Choose Your Companion — Pick from 4 distinct starter pets, each bringing unique advantages and flavor to your journey.
  • Rachambo Combat — Battle rival pets in strategic Rock-Paper-Scissors style duels where smart reads and timing mean everything.
  • Over 15 Unique Opponents — Face off against a diverse roster of enemy pets, each with their own special abilities and tricks up their sleeves.
  • The Re-Hamstered Edition — Experience the definitive version of the game featuring rebalanced matchups, quality-of-life improvements, and refined gameplay post-launch.
