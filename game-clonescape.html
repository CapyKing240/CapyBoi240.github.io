Clonescape

Awaken as a clone in a high-tech facility and test your wits against deadly traps, intricate switches, and precision platforming challenges to escape.

Play on itch.io →

About the Game

Clonescape drops you into the heart of a strange, high-tech testing facility after you awaken inside a mysterious containment capsule. Created in a hidden lab, a sudden explosion frees you from your chamber, leaving you trapped in an unfamiliar labyrinth guarded by vigilant turrets, tricky switches, and lethal environmental hazards.

Armed only with your wits and agility, every room becomes a test of your survival. Leap across dangerous platforms, flip complex switches, and dodge defensive systems as you navigate the maze. As you push deeper into the facility, questions nag at your resolve: Why were you created? What secrets does this lab hold? And can you unravel the truth before the facility stops you for good?

Key Features

  • Intricate Puzzle Rooms — Flip switches, trigger mechanisms, and clear complex paths to navigate through each room of the facility.
  • A Sci-Fi Escape Story — Piece together why you were created and uncover the secrets behind the lab's sudden destruction.
  • Precision Platforming — Tight, responsive controls designed for precise jumps, fast timing, and dodging lethal hazards.
  • Vigilant Defenses — Outsmart active turrets and environmental traps that test both your timing and reflexes.
